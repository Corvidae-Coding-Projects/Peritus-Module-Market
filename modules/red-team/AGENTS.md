# modules/red-team — navigation

Hardened source-only Red Team module. It exposes durable contracts, host-enforced scoped-source
capture, retained observations, contained proof jobs, digest-pinned dependency preparation,
bounded autonomous discovery, independent skeptical adjudication, honest reports, and a pinned
module-store source-build template. Confirmed findings advance automatically through copy-only
root-cause patching, replay matrices, independent patch review, original-byte verification, and the
complete generic TUI/WebUI action, progress, diff, summary, and export projection. Its adversarial
corpus preserves containment, deception, lifecycle, and resource-pressure cases used by the final
bounded acceptance campaign.
